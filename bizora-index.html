ABOUT BIZORA

Independent. Informative. Transparent.

BIZORA is designed as an independent discovery platform for job information and businesses. It is not affiliated with any government department or employer unless explicitly stated and authorised.

Information & verification

Listings should show their original source and a last-checked date. Job statuses can change quickly; an “Open” label should only be used after checking the official source. If a status cannot be confirmed, show “Awaiting verification.”

Corrections & removal

Businesses and visitors should be able to report incorrect information or request removal. Before the public launch, add a working contact channel and a process to review these requests.
