About BIZORA

A simpler way to discover India's businesses.

BIZORA is being built to make business discovery easier. Our goal is to help people find relevant services and give businesses a clear place to present their offerings.

Our approach

BIZORA is an evolving project. A listing is not an endorsement or a guarantee of service quality. Visitors should verify important details directly with a business before making decisions.

For business owners

Only submit information you are authorized to share. Avoid including private personal information. You can request a correction or removal of a listing by contacting the platform owner.
